Offering 02 · Leadership Programming & Human Performance

Built to change how your people lead, think, and perform.

We do not teach leadership — we build it, the way Special Operations builds people. When your organization needs people who can think clearly under pressure, lead without a script, work as a team, and perform when it counts.

Select a Program Area
Leadership Programming & Human Performance · 001

Leader & Team Development

How elite teams are built.

SOCOM does not build elite teams by accident. It is a deliberate process — identifying the unique strengths each person brings, building trust through shared adversity, and creating the kind of cohesion that holds when things go wrong.

ALLTRU brings that same methodology to your leadership team or department. This is not a trust-fall exercise. It is a structured process that helps your team understand how they actually operate under pressure — and what is getting in the way of performing at the level they are capable of.

Format
  • Half-day or full-day sessions for intact teams
  • Can include team assessments, structured exercises, and facilitated debriefs
  • Scales from a single leadership team to multiple departments
Solves For

Team cohesion, trust-building, communication breakdowns, performance under pressure.

Leadership Programming & Human Performance · 002

Women's Executive Coaching

One-on-one or group. Built for female executives.

Executive coaching from ALLTRU is not theory delivered by someone who studied leadership. It is direct, candid, and built on real experience navigating male-dominated, high-stakes environments — and rewriting what leadership looks like.

Each engagement is paired with an ALLTRU operator matched to the executive's specific challenges — whether that is navigating a high-stakes transition, building credibility in a room that was not designed for her, or making decisions under pressure.

Format
  • 1:1 or small-group sessions, virtual or in-person
  • Delivered as a series (4, 8, or 12 sessions) or an ongoing retainer relationship
  • Standalone or integrated into any Leadership Programming & Human Performance engagement
Solves For

Decision-making under pressure, executive presence, navigating organizational politics, building an authentic leadership voice as a woman in the room.

Corporate Offsites

These are not corporate retreats with a ropes course bolted on. Leadership Programming & Human Performance · 005

Cultural Assessments

See your culture as it actually operates.

Every leadership program works better when you know what you're actually dealing with. ALLTRU's cultural assessments are built the way our operators assess any environment before acting on it — direct observation, structured interviews, and an honest read on trust, friction, and psychological safety across your organization.

We deliver findings your team can act on immediately, not a binder that sits on a shelf.

Format
  • Lunch & Learn — an introductory session on the assessment framework and early observations
  • One-Day Full Assessment & Offsite — structured assessment delivered on-site with the full team
  • Six- to Twelve-Month Cultural Deep Dive — surveys, interviews, and ongoing recommendations

Diagnosing trust and friction points before they become retention problems, benchmarking culture ahead of a leadership program, giving leadership an honest, evidence-based read on how the organization actually operates.

Offering 03 · Advisory

Advisory, built around four distinct needs.

Corporate clients engage ALLTRU Advisory for one of four reasons: to enter the federal market, build trust with mission owners, prove a product actually fits a real mission, or win a specific pursuit. Each pathway is led by named advisors — operators who've served inside Special Operations and the intelligence community, and product leaders who've built at major technology companies.

Select a Pathway
Advisory · 001

Federal Market Entry & Go-to-Market Strategy

Fixed-Scope Strategy Engagement

The defense market doesn't reward good technology. It rewards technology that speaks its language.

Most commercial playbooks fail the moment they meet a government procurement process. ALLTRU helps you find the entry point — translating your capability into the language federal buyers evaluate against, and identifying where your first realistic win actually is. Led by advisors who've generated requirements from inside government and product leaders who know how to translate technical capability into a case a program office will act on.

For our clients, this pathway answers: Where do we go, and how do we get in?

What We Do
  • Acquisition pathway assessment
  • Capability-to-requirement translation
  • Entry point and beachhead strategy
  • Early-stage positioning review
Solves For

Shortening the runway from commercial technology to a first funded federal engagement. Avoiding the translation failures that stall promising companies before they ever reach a program office.

Advisory · 002

Left-of-Bang Stakeholder Shaping & Advisory

Standing Advisory Retainer

Trust with mission owners takes years to build. We bring what already exists.

A standing advisory retainer with a small number of named, screened advisors — senior leaders who've operated inside DoD, DOE/NNSA, DOS, and the interagency community — engaged to build understanding and credibility with program offices and mission owners before a solicitation exists. Every advisor is cleared for engagement before activation, and advisors include former government leaders who've sat inside these conversations themselves, so shaping is substantively informed, not just door-opening.

For our clients, this pathway answers: Who needs to trust us before we need them to?

What We Do
  • Named advisor engagement
  • Mission-owner and program-office relationship building
  • Strategic positioning ahead of formal pursuits
  • Curated advisory cadence
Solves For

Building trust that can't be manufactured in a 90-day sales cycle. Entering a pursuit already known to the people who matter, not cold.

Advisory · 003

Use Case & Product-Market Fit Strategy

Fixed-Scope Engagement, Recurring Validation Cycle

Fit isn't assumed. It's proven.

ALLTRU's product and mission-strategy bench includes advisors who built requirements from inside government program offices and led tech scouting to close capability gaps, alongside product leaders with years of product management experience at major technology companies. Together they pressure-test whether a capability genuinely solves a real mission problem, and translate that fit into language a program office and an evaluator will both recognize.

For our clients, this pathway answers: Does what we have actually fit what they need, and how do we prove it?

What We Do
  • End-user validation panels
  • Use-case and mission-thread mapping
  • Requirements-gap analysis
  • Positioning and narrative refinement
Solves For

Knowing whether a capability actually fits a mission before investing in a pursuit. Turning validated fit into positioning both a program office and a source-selection evaluator will recognize as legitimate.

Advisory · 004

Capture & Proposal Strategy Advisory

Per-Pursuit Engagement

Winning a federal contract is a discipline, not a network.

This pathway includes hands-on capture support for a live pursuit — pricing strategy, contract vehicle selection, proposal governance, and win-theme development — from advisors who've built and closed federal awards themselves, not just observed them. Win themes draw on both capture professionals and product strategists, so proposals hold up on genuine technical differentiation, not administrative polish alone.

For our clients, this pathway answers: How do we win this specific pursuit?

What We Do
  • Pricing and contract vehicle strategy (OTA, IDIQ, STRATFI/TACFI)
  • Proposal governance and win-theme development
  • Bid/no-bid and capture planning support
  • Named advisors with direct capture track records
Solves For

Turning a qualified opportunity into a signed award. Avoiding the pricing and positioning mistakes that sink proposals after the relationship work is already done.

Every pathway is led by named advisors matched to your specific need — not a blanket claim on the full roster.
